Withdraw From Contract

If you have changed your mind about an eligible online order, this page is how you tell us that you wish to withdraw from the contract, under your statutory right of withdrawal. Completing the form below is the quickest way to notify us — you do not need an account or to log in.

Start your withdrawal request →

When can I withdraw?

You have 14 days from the day you (or someone you nominate) receives your order to tell us you wish to withdraw — you do not need to give a reason. You then have a further 14 days from the date you give us notice to return the goods to us.

The right of withdrawal does not apply to certain goods once purchased, including:

  • Sealed health, hygiene or cosmetic products which have been opened after delivery
  • Medicines and other pharmacy-supplied medicinal products
  • Perishable or short-shelf-life goods
  • Goods made to your specification or clearly personalised

This does not affect your statutory rights in relation to goods that are faulty, damaged, or not as described.

[PLACEHOLDER — end]

Returning the goods

Goods should be returned unused, unopened where applicable, in their original packaging, and in a condition suitable for resale. You are responsible for the cost of return postage, unless the item is faulty, damaged or was sent to you incorrectly. We recommend using a tracked delivery service, as we cannot take responsibility for items lost in transit back to us.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to your original payment method. We may withhold reimbursement until we have received the goods back, or you have supplied evidence of having sent them back, whichever is earlier.
