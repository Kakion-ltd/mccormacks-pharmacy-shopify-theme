About CeraVe

CeraVe was developed in 2005 with dermatologists to address a simple problem: many cleansers strip the skin of the very lipids that keep it healthy. Every CeraVe product contains three essential ceramides — the same lipids found naturally in the skin barrier — alongside hyaluronic acid to help hold moisture.

The range is fragrance-free, non-comedogenic and suitable for sensitive skin, which is why it is one of the most commonly recommended brands by dermatologists and pharmacists alike. If you are not sure which cleanser or moisturiser suits your skin, ask our team — the differences between the blue, green and white bottles matter more than the packaging suggests.
