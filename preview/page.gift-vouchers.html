About McCormack’s gift vouchers

A McCormack’s gift voucher works across everything we sell — pharmacy and healthcare, vitamins and supplements, skincare, beauty, fragrance, baby and toiletries — online and in all seven of our stores. It is a practical present for someone who is particular about their skincare, restocking after a new baby, or simply hard to buy for. Email vouchers arrive within minutes and can be scheduled for a specific date. Printed vouchers are posted in a card, free of charge, within Ireland. Vouchers are valid for five years from the date of purchase, can be spent over as many visits as you like, and any remaining balance stays on the voucher.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a gift voucher valid for?

Five years from the date of purchase. The expiry date is printed on the voucher and shown on the email.

Can it be spent online and in store?

Yes — online at checkout, and at the counter in any of our seven stores. The same voucher works in both.

What happens if they spend less than the full amount?

Any remaining balance stays on the voucher and can be used on as many future visits as they like, until it runs out or expires.

Can I add to a voucher or use more than one on an order?

Vouchers cannot be topped up, but you can use several on a single order. If the order comes to more than the voucher balance, pay the difference by card as normal.

What if the voucher is lost?

Email vouchers can be resent to the original recipient address — contact us with your order number. Printed vouchers work like cash and cannot be replaced if lost.

Are gift vouchers refundable?

Vouchers cannot be returned or exchanged for cash, in line with Irish consumer law on gift vouchers. Products bought with a voucher can be returned under our normal returns policy.
