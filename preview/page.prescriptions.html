Good to know

We hold your prescription on file and dispense each repeat as it falls due. Please allow two working days, and order before you run out — particularly around bank holidays.

If your prescription has expired, or your GP has changed your dose, we will need a new prescription before we can dispense. We will tell you as soon as we spot it.
