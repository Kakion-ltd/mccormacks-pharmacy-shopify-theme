Pharmacy & pharmacist-reviewed items

Certain healthcare and pharmacy-only products require review by our pharmacy team before they can be dispatched. This may add a short delay. Our pharmacist may contact you before supplying an item, and may decline to supply it where it would not be clinically appropriate to do so. Where we decline to supply a product, we refund you in full for that item.

Incorrect delivery addresses

It is your responsibility to make sure the delivery address you give at checkout is correct and complete. Please check it before you place your order. Because we prioritise fast dispatch, it may not be possible to change delivery details after an order is placed — contact us straight away and we will do what we can.

If an address is incorrect or incomplete and your parcel is returned to us, we will resend it once the additional delivery charge has been paid.
