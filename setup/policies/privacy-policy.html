Privacy Policy

This is the Privacy Policy for McCormack's Pharmacy and applies to this website. Its purpose is to give you an understanding of the information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Who we are

Melfer Limited, trading as McCormack's Pharmacy, 23 Bolton Street, Clonmel, Co. Tipperary, E91 E0F1, is the data controller for the information described in this policy. Company registration number: 509318.

You can contact us about privacy at info@mccormackspharmacy.ie or on 052 6199329. For data protection matters, contact our Superintendent Pharmacist, Fergal McCormack, at info@mccormackspharmacy.ie.

Who can use this website

  • You must be over 18 to register for an account, place an order, or subscribe to our email newsletters.
  • We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under the age of 18.
  • The information on this website is for guidance and information only, and should not take the place of a diagnosis by your general practitioner or local pharmacist.

The information we collect

Personal data is information that can be used to identify you: your name, email address, billing and delivery addresses, and telephone numbers.

When you register an account, we collect your email address, first and last name, address details, phone number, password (this is encrypted and is not visible to us), order history and wish list.

When you place an order, we collect your email address, first and last name, address details, mobile number and the details of your order.

Health information. If you submit prescription details to us, ask our pharmacist a question, or answer the pharmacist's questions on a medicine product, we collect the health information you give us. We treat this as special category data, we use it only to assess and fulfil your request safely, and access to it is limited to the pharmacy team who need it.

Our prescription forms do not accept file uploads. We collect the details you type in, and we contact you by phone or email to arrange dispensing. Prescription medicines are not sold online — they are collected in person or delivered to you after our pharmacist has verified the prescription.

Non-personal data is statistical information about how visitors use the site, which cannot be used to identify or contact you.

Why we collect it

We use your personal data:

  • to supply the goods you have ordered, which involves sharing your delivery details with our courier;
  • to contact you about your order;
  • to respond to your messages and enquiries;
  • to meet our legal and professional obligations as a pharmacy, including keeping a record of each online medicine transaction for two years as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland requires;
  • where you have asked us to, to tell you about promotions, events and similar.

We use non-personal data to understand how the site is used, to improve it, and to estimate audience size and usage patterns.

Our legal bases

We rely on the performance of our contract with you to process orders; on your consent for marketing email and for non-essential cookies; on our legal obligations for pharmacy record-keeping and tax; and on our legitimate interests in running and improving the website securely. Where we process health information, we do so for the purposes of your healthcare, handled by or under the responsibility of a pharmacist bound by professional confidentiality.

Who we share it with

  • Shopify, which provides and hosts this online store and holds our customer and order records.
  • Shopify Payments, which processes card payments, including those made through Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay, and PayPal, if you choose to pay that way. We never see or store your full card details — these go directly to the payment provider.
  • An Post and DPD, our delivery carriers, who receive the name, address and contact details needed to deliver your parcel.
  • Professional advisers and regulators, where we are required to share information.

We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies and tracking

Our Cookie Policy sets out exactly which cookies this site sets, what each one does and how long it lasts. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner, and change your mind at any time.

How long we keep it

Online medicine transaction records are kept for two years, as the PSI requires. Other order records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Everything else is kept only as long as it's needed for the purpose it was collected for, and then deleted.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, where we are not required to keep it;
  • receive your data in a portable form;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, our processing of your data;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, including by using the unsubscribe link at the foot of every marketing email.

To exercise any of these, email info@mccormackspharmacy.ie. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, 6 Pembroke Row, Dublin 2, D02 XW46 — www.dataprotection.ie.
