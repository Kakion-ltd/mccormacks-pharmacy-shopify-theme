# Product images — what we need from you

The product image work is finished. 96% of the products in your store now have
a high-resolution image in place of the small, blurred thumbnails that were
there before.

70 products that were on the pharmacy list but missing from the store have also
been added, with their images. They are not on the website yet: the pharmacy is
deciding when they go on. A 71st turned out to be a second copy of a product
already in the store, and was removed.

Three things are left, and each one needs a decision from you rather than more
work from us. They are set out below, and the spreadsheet that came with this
document has a tab for each.

Only the orange columns in the spreadsheet need filling in. Most of them are
dropdowns.

description and barcode, but stock is not tracked on them yet. That needs
setting, with real figures, before they go on the website, or they could be
ordered whatever is on the shelf.
